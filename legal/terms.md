## What Pumpwise is

Pumpwise shows fuel prices that stations report to official government price schemes in Australia, Spain, France, Portugal and Mexico. It is free, has no accounts and is not affiliated with any government, station or fuel brand.

The prices are there for your own driving. Each government lets its prices be reused on its own terms, listed under "Data sources" below and at https://tjcraw6589.github.io/pumpwise/data-licences.html. One exception: Queensland's licence does not allow its prices to be published again, so please do not copy or redistribute Queensland prices.

## Prices can be wrong

Stations report their own prices, and Pumpwise collects them from each official source regularly, from every few minutes to every few hours depending on the country. Stations sometimes report mistakes, and a price can change after it was reported. Always check the price at the pump before you fill up. In Western Australia you can contact FuelWatch at https://www.fuelwatch.wa.gov.au/contact if a station is not selling at its published price.

Opening hours, facilities, restrictions and locations also come from the stations and may be out of date. Distances are in a straight line, so the drive will usually be longer. "Best value" and savings are estimates based on the fuel economy and fill-up size you choose.

## Use it safely

Do not use Pumpwise while driving. Let a passenger check, or look before you set off. Follow the road, traffic signs and the law at all times; directions are provided by the maps app you choose.
