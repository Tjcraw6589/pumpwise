# Price pages: what was fixed (12 Nov 2026)

Covers WEBSITE_FIXES_PROMPT.md (six testers) and the open items of PRICE_SITE_UI_BRIEF.md. Pictures in this
folder: `before-*` is the live site as it was, `after-*` the new build, each at 390 and 1440 wide, light and dark.

Proof run on a local build of the live feed of 12 Nov 2026 (`npm run prices`, then
`node scripts/prices-site/check.mjs`): 23 pages (home, 404, every region page with its largest and smallest
place) at 360, 390, 768 and 1440 wide, light and dark. No page wider than the screen, every link, button and
field at least 44 by 44 on phones, every text pair at least 4.5 to 1, the first station row on the first
screen of every place page at 360 and 390, search and "Near me" working, no text left in another language.

## Independent review

Three reviewers read the change and a skeptic checked each claim. Eleven real problems were found and fixed
before the push: full Portuguese postcodes found nothing; one failed download stopped the search until a
reload; "Near me" fuel chips did nothing when the browser keeps no storage; "Near me" stayed in the old
language after a switch; same named Mexican towns had no link from "Near me"; a feed name starting with "@"
could turn into page text; a failed price download said the location could not be found; Mexican stations
just outside a city's circle (Puebla) were on no page; three letter brands (IOR, OTR) lost their capitals; a
"|" inside a town name split it in the search; and INEGI's credit was missing from the data licences page.
