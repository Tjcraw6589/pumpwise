What it does

  • Shows the nearest station and the cheapest one inside the area you choose, with directions in one tap.
  • Counts the fuel you'd burn getting there, so you can see whether the cheaper station is worth the drive.
  • In Western Australia, see tomorrow's prices after 2:30pm, so you know whether to fill up today or wait.
  • Free, with no account to make. The app has no adverts.

Support

If a Western Australian station isn't selling at its published price, contact FuelWatch.

Where the prices come from

Western Australia: prices from FuelWatch, Government of Western Australia. Every station's price is fixed for 24 hours from 6am, and tomorrow's prices are published by 2:30pm.

Pumpwise adds a new place whenever a government publishes its station prices for free. It is not affiliated with any government, station or fuel brand.
