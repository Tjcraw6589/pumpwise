Common questions

Where does Pumpwise work?

The website covers Western Australia, New South Wales and the ACT, Tasmania, Spain, France, Mexico and Portugal. The iPhone app covers Western Australia for now.

A price looks wrong. What can I do?

Prices are the ones stations report to each official service, so always check the price at the pump. If a station in Western Australia is not selling at its published price, FuelWatch can help.

Contact FuelWatch

Does Pumpwise track me?

Pumpwise has no accounts, and your location stays on your phone or in your browser. The website shows Google adverts, which may use cookies; visitors in Europe and the UK are asked first. The app has no adverts.

Read the privacy policy

Why is my country or state missing?

Pumpwise adds a place when its government publishes station prices that anyone may use for free. If you know of one, send an email.
