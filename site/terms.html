What Pumpwise is

Pumpwise shows fuel prices reported by UK forecourts to the UK Government Fuel Finder scheme. It is free, has no accounts and is not affiliated with the UK Government, any forecourt or any fuel brand.

Prices can be wrong

Forecourts report their own prices, and Pumpwise collects them about every 15 minutes. A price can change after it was reported, and forecourts sometimes report mistakes. Always check the price at the pump before you fill up. If a price is wrong, you can report it to GOV.UK at https://www.gov.uk/guidance/report-an-error-in-fuel-prices-or-forecourt-details.

Opening hours, facilities and locations also come from the forecourts and may be out of date. Distances are in a straight line, so the drive will usually be longer. "Best value" estimates are a guide based on the miles per gallon and fill-up size you choose.

Use it safely

Do not use Pumpwise while driving. Let a passenger check, or look before you set off. Follow the road, traffic signs and the law at all times; directions are provided by the maps app you choose.
