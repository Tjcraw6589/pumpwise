What Pumpwise is

Pumpwise shows fuel prices that stations report to official government price schemes, in Australia, Europe and Mexico. It is free, has no accounts and is not affiliated with any government, station or fuel brand.

Prices can be wrong

Stations report their own prices, and Pumpwise collects them from the official source about once an hour (more often where prices change during the day). Stations sometimes report mistakes, and a price can change after it was reported. Always check the price at the pump before you fill up. In Western Australia you can contact FuelWatch at https://www.fuelwatch.wa.gov.au/contact if a station is not selling at its published price.

Opening hours, facilities, restrictions and locations also come from the stations and may be out of date. Distances are in a straight line, so the drive will usually be longer. "Best value" and savings are estimates based on the fuel economy and fill-up size you choose.

Use it safely

Do not use Pumpwise while driving. Let a passenger check, or look before you set off. Follow the road, traffic signs and the law at all times; directions are provided by the maps app you choose.

Data sources

  • Western Australia: prices from FuelWatch, Government of Western Australia, www.fuelwatch.wa.gov.au. FuelWatch allows its data to be used in apps with this acknowledgement and link.
  • New South Wales and the Australian Capital Territory: prices from FuelCheck, NSW Government (Creative Commons Attribution ShareAlike 3.0 Australia).
  • Tasmania: prices from FuelCheck TAS, Tasmanian Government.
  • Spain: prices from the Geoportal de Gasolineras, Ministerio para la Transición Ecológica y el Reto Demográfico, Government of Spain (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0).
  • France: prices from prix-carburants.gouv.fr, Ministère de l'Économie (Licence Ouverte 2.0). Each price shows when it was last updated.
  • Portugal: prices from the Direção-Geral de Energia e Geologia (DGEG), Government of Portugal. Each price shows when it was last updated.
  • Mexico: "Precios de gasolinas y diésel reportados por los permisionarios", Comisión Nacional de Energía (formerly the CRE), datos.gob.mx (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0). Town names: INEGI, Marco Geoestadístico 2024.
  • Slovenia: source goriva.si, the fuel price portal of the Republic of Slovenia.
  • Croatia: source Ministry of Economy of the Republic of Croatia, minGO fuel price portal. Pumpwise is not an official service of the Ministry.
  • United Kingdom (when available): contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0 (https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).

Pumpwise changes no prices; it only reformats them. The full licence details are at https://pumpwiseapp.com/data-licences.html.
