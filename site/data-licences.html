Where the Pumpwise prices come from, and how you may reuse them

The Pumpwise price feed (the data/ folders on this site) is a copy of official government price lists in a different shape, rebuilt every 15 minutes. Every meta.json carries a licences section with the same information as this page. Pumpwise is not affiliated with any government, station or fuel brand.

Western Australia

Owner
Government of Western Australia, FuelWatch, www.fuelwatch.wa.gov.au
Terms
FuelWatch's free RSS feed for reuse (tools/rss): acknowledge FuelWatch with a link. There is no Creative Commons licence; reuse this part of the feed on FuelWatch's own terms, with the same credit.

Prices from FuelWatch, Government of Western Australia.

Tasmania
